LEVEL-2 UNIT OF ANALYSIS


Define and record the Phase-5 Level-2 unit of analysis before enumeration.


A Level-2 candidate is one independently inspectable child mechanism, operation, transition, dependency, predicate, ownership edge, lifecycle segment, commit point, persistence mechanism, collision domain, reentrancy surface, publication mechanism, readiness dependency, or comparable finer-grained object that is:

- directly beneath an `ELIGIBLE` Level-1 parent boundary/object;
- exactly one resolution finer than the Level-1 unit;
- supported by current repository or artifact evidence;
- distinguishable from siblings by behavior, ownership, lifecycle, contract, or failure semantics; and
- independently capable of receiving a later Level-2 correctness disposition.


Apply this resolution test:

- Level 1: system, subsystem, major lifecycle, broad contract, or major mechanism question.
- Level 2: an independently inspectable mechanism within that Level-1 parent.
- Level 3 or deeper: implementation-edge or microscopic decomposition beneath the Level-2 mechanism.


Do not remain at Level-1 abstraction because decomposition is difficult. Do not enumerate statements, individual syntax, or implementation fragments merely because they exist. When uncertain whether an object is Level 2 or deeper, state the test and evidence, preserve the coarser valid Level-2 object, and do not prematurely decompose it.
